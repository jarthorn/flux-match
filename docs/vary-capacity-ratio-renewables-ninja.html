Flux Match Experiment: Varying VRE Capacity Ratios

In this experiment, we vary the solar and wind capacity in each balancing area across Canada, and observe the impact on net demand, curtailment, and satisfied demand hours. The capacity ratio is the ratio of solar or wind nameplate capacity in each balancing area vs average hourly demand in that balancing area. For example, a solar capacity ratio of 2 means the solar nameplate capacity is twice the average hourly demand in each balancing area. Results are averaged across all balancing areas in Canada. Results are calculated using Flux Match, a very simple dispatch model for the Canadian electricity system. Demand and solar capacity factors are taken from the CODERS database; wind capacity factors are from renewables.ninja.
