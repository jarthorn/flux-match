Flux Match

Flux Match is a simple dispatch model that simulates variable generation (wind, solar, and batteries) against historical hourly electricity demand across Canadian balancing areas. Balancing areas represent grid planning regions, which in most cases correspond to a single province, with Ontario and Quebec split into two balancing areas each. Generation is based on a hypothetical allocation of wind and solar sites chosen from map grid cells; the model measures how well those choices cover real demand curves. The model is deliberately simplistic: it excludes economic factors such as grid connection costs, and excludes other energy sources such as fossil fuels, hydro, and nuclear. The model's purpose is to focus on the variability of wind and solar power, and how site selection strategies can be used to mitigate variability and better match variable supply with demand.

Demand and capacity-factor data come from the CODERS database, unless otherwise noted. Source code is on GitHub.
